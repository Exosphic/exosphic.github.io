Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ page.last_updated | date: '%-d %B %Y' }}

Exosphic is an independently operated software development practice based in Sri Lanka. This policy explains how we handle information you send through this website or by email. For questions about this policy, contact {{ site.email }}.

Information you provide

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, and message. You may also provide a company name. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form. If you email us directly, we receive the information you include in your email.

How we use it

We use inquiry details to reply to you, discuss your request, and communicate with you about a potential or ongoing project. We do not use these details for mailing lists or unrelated marketing, and we do not sell them.

Form delivery and storage

The contact form sends submissions to Formspree, which processes the message for delivery to our email and may also retain it in its submission dashboard under its own policies and settings. We keep the delivered copy in email. Our website does not maintain a separate database of inquiries. We do not share inquiry details with unrelated parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and analytics

We currently do not use analytics or advertising tracking tools on this website, and we do not set tracking cookies. The hosting, form, and email services needed to operate the site may process technical information under their own policies. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy before using it.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed to handle an inquiry or any resulting project, and for any applicable recordkeeping needs. You can email {{ site.email }} to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent us. We will review the request and address records within our control, subject to any applicable obligations.

Changes

We may update this policy as our website and practices change. The date above shows when this version was last updated.
